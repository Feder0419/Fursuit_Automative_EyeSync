## Question to be Discuss
1. LED Display: We want the fursuit head to have an eye pattern even if the whole system is powered off. Should we use certain display like e-Ink display or should we just imprint a physical pattern on the LED display. When LED is powered off, the pattern will show itself, but when LED is powered on, it will overlap and block the pattern.
2. Maybe we should give an option that the fursuit's escort could control it for the wearer.
3. It's a good thought that the LED display could have a stabalizer function. As we know, fursuiter dance and shake their heads a lot, so it would be good if their line of sight stay within a small range (not staying completely still, that would seem too rigid). The difficulty of this task is fine tuning, to make the function as natural as possible.
4. Be aware of its brightness and refresh rate so it would be camera friendly. Also it would be good if the display can automatically adjust its brightness according to environment lighting. Also put a diffuser layer / coating over the display to smooth out the image and reduce reflection from camera flash light.
5. A lot of current LED eye products are laggy and has low refresh rate.
